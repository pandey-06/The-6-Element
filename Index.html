WHAT THE MODEL WATCHES

THE 6 ELEMENTS

Every station's cracking probability is built from six live signals — each tracked, colour-coded and weighted differently in the risk model below.

🌧️
01 · WEIGHT 35%
Rainfall
Live hourly precipitation at each station's real coordinates.
🟤
02 · WEIGHT 25%
Soil Moisture
Simulated saturation of slope soil, rising with sustained rain.
📳
03 · WEIGHT 20%
Ground Vibration
Simulated micro-tremor and settling activity at the slope.
⛰️
04 · WEIGHT 20%
Slope Angle
Fixed terrain steepness for each monitored zone.
🌡️
05 · CONTEXT
Temperature
Live surface temperature, tracked for trend context.
💧
06 · CONTEXT
Humidity
Live relative humidity, an early signal of saturating air.
